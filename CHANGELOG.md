## 1.0.0

- Initial release, generated from [Majesticons v2.1.3](https://github.com/halfmage/majesticons/releases/tag/v2.1.3).
- 380 icons in two styles: `MajesticonsLine` and `MajesticonsSolid`.
- `Majesticon` widget that follows `IconTheme`, like Flutter's `Icon`.
- `MajesticonCategory` and the `majesticons` list to browse the 380 icons by category.
- `tool/generate.dart` to regenerate the icons from any upstream tag.
